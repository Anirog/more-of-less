Have you ever set a simple profile picture on your iPhone, only for it to disappear or turn into a full-screen poster when you call someone? I have, and I spent far too long trying to work out why my phone would not just show a normal circular photo.

It kept falling back to my initials or filling the whole screen with my face.

What I eventually worked out is that Apple links the small circular photo to the full-screen "Contact Poster". If you turn sharing off completely for privacy, the phone hides the photo as well and shows your initials instead.

## If you just want a normal photo without the giant poster

1. **Turn sharing on, but make it ask.** Open your contact card, tap **Shared Name and Photo**, and turn it on. Set it to **Always Ask** so your phone can use the photo without sharing it automatically.
2. **Set your avatar.** Tap **Edit** under your photo, choose the **Avatar** tab at the top, then select the picture you want.
3. **Delete the poster.** If the full-screen background still appears, swipe up on it and delete it.

It feels backwards, but it worked for me. I now have the small profile picture I wanted without my face filling the screen every time I make a call.
